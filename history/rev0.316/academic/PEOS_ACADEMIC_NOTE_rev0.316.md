# Qualitative relationship policy and evidential restraint

Closeness and tolerance for informal jokes are separate dimensions. A verified family relationship can fix the former without removing topic boundaries or proving the latter. A display name supplies neither relationship authentication nor access authority.

Qualitative conditions preserve a conjunction, exceptions and correction paths without inventing a numerical score. The reference policy consumes explicitly typed context; it does not classify natural language or establish psychological states. Recovery requires relevant evidence and an explicit review, not an invented timeout or automatic point threshold.

Metaphor, hypothesis and reported events have different evidential effects. A correction can retract a mistaken assistant inference while preserving the historical words. Background information can add reasons without retracting a joke. These are testable contracts; synthetic tests do not establish successful personality inheritance or a model's pragmatic competence.
